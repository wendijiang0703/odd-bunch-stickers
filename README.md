# Odd Bunch Sticker Station

A student prototype (RCA / Imperial IDE 2 group project, "Odd Bunch") that turns wonky, out-of-spec
vegetables into little characters for their packaging, so the odd one is the fun one to pick.

**Try it:** https://wendijiang.com/odd-bunch-stickers/

## How to test

1. Open the link on a laptop (Chrome, Safari or Edge). The first visit downloads the vision engine
   (about 10 MB), so give it a few seconds.
2. Click a photo in **Test set**, load your own photo, or turn the camera on and press **Capture**.
   Best results: one vegetable on a plain white surface, photographed from above.
3. Without a key you see hand-made demo results for the test photos. To let the AI make its own
   character, click the **AI** lamp at the top left and paste a free Gemini key from
   [aistudio.google.com/apikey](https://aistudio.google.com/apikey).
4. Drag the stickers to adjust them, then **Save PNG** or **Print** (50 × 70 mm label).

## Privacy

Everything runs in your browser. Your key is stored only in your own browser and is sent only to
Google. Photos are sent only to Google's Gemini API, and only after you add a key. Nothing is sent
to us and nothing is stored on a server. On Gemini's free tier, Google may use what is sent to
improve its products, so don't photograph anything private.
